PANEER SABJI

Ingredients

For Tampering
  • 3 tablespoon Oil or Ghee
  • 1 teaspoon Shahi Jeera
  • 1 piece Cinnamon
  • 2 pods Whole Cardamom crushed
  • 2 no Whole Cloves
For Gravy
  • 200 grams Paneer cubed
  • 1 tablespoon Kashmiri Chilli Powder
  • 2 teaspoon Garam Masala Powder
  • ¼ cup Yogurt | Curd thick and unsour
  • ¼ cup Cream
  • 2 teaspoon Salt or to taste
  • 2 teaspoon Sugar or to taste
  • 4 tablespoon Coriander Leaves finely chopped
For Boiling & Grinding
  • 2 large Onions peeled & chopped
  • 1 inch Ginger peeled & chopped
  • 6 cloves Garlic peeled & chopped
  • 15 no Cashews
  • 1 cup Water

Instructions

  1. Take cubed paneer in a bowl, add hot water over it and let it sit till needed.
  2. In a pan, take onion and other ingredients given for grinding. Cook that for 10 mins. Now cool this and take it in a blender. Puree till smooth. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a kadai. Add in whole spices and saute for a min. Add in pureed onion mix and cook till it thickens. Add in chilli and garam masala powder and give a good mix.
  4. Pour whisked yogurt and mix well. Cook for few mins. Add in water and bring it to a boil. Add in drained paneer and simmer for few mins. Add in salt, sugar and cream to taste and mix well. Add in coriander leaves and mix well and serve.
